# 🏔️ TrekPlan

### Plan Your Journey. Manage Your Budget. Explore With Confidence.

TrekPlan is a **travel and trekking planning website** designed to help travelers explore destinations, understand trek details, and organize their trips from one place.

The project is being developed step-by-step using **HTML, CSS and JavaScript**, with a focus on creating a simple, clean and practical travel-planning experience.

> 🚧 **Status: Currently under development**

---

## 🌍 About TrekPlan

TrekPlan was inspired by my interest in **travelling and trekking**.

While planning a trip, information about destinations, difficulty, distance, budget, things to carry and other details is often spread across different platforms.

The goal of TrekPlan is to bring these important details together and make trip planning easier and more organized.

### The main idea:

**Where should I go? → What can I do there? → How difficult is it? → How much can it cost? → How can I plan my trip?**

## 🎯 Project Goal

The long-term goal of TrekPlan is to create a platform where travelers can manage the important parts of their journey from one place.

Instead of switching between multiple websites for destinations, trek information, budgets, transportation, accommodation and preparation, TrekPlan aims to bring these parts together into a single workspace.

### TrekPlan focuses on:

**Explore → Understand → Plan → Manage → Travel**
